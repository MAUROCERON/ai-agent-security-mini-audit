Handoff QA For Client Agents

A USD 59 fixed-scope review for one client workflow before you hand it over. Built for agencies shipping AI agents, n8n/Make/Zapier automations, browser workflows, CRM updates, support agents or lead qualification systems.

Use it before delivery when

  • The agent can update CRM records, send messages, create tasks, export data or trigger follow-up.
  • The workflow reads untrusted emails, tickets, forms, web pages, PDFs or customer notes.
  • The automation uses browser sessions, Playwright, screenshots, downloads, uploads or clipboard data.
  • The client may ask: "What security checks did you run before handoff?"

What the agency gets

  • Launch verdict for one workflow: ship, ship after fixes, or stop until a high-risk gap is closed.
  • Top findings with evidence, severity, OWASP Agentic/LLM mapping and NIST AI 600-1 themes.
  • One to three copy-paste remediation tickets you can assign before delivery.
  • Retest steps to confirm the first fixes.
  • One first-fix retest reply within 7 days after you send redacted fix evidence.
  • A short client-safe security note you can include in the handoff packet.
  • A client-facing QA line-item template for proposals, invoices or delivery notes.

Why agencies can resell it

The review is small on purpose. It is not a full pentest or compliance audit. It is a practical external QA layer for the obvious expensive mistakes: over-scoped tokens, missing approval gates, unsafe browser sessions, sensitive logs and untrusted text steering tool calls.

Simple add-on math

Agencies can position this as a fixed-scope external handoff QA add-on. You may pass through the USD 59 price, bundle it into your delivery fee, or charge a separate USD 99-149 client QA line item when the workflow touches customer data, CRM writes, browser sessions or outbound messages.

Keep the scope honest: this is not certification, legal advice or a full penetration test. It is a launch-decision report for one workflow, with the first fix and retest path made clear before client handoff.

The paid handoff pack also includes conservative wording you can paste into a proposal, invoice or delivery note so the client sees why the QA line item exists.

Resale acceptance criteria

  • The client can see a clear launch decision for one workflow, not a vague security opinion.
  • The agency gets one first fix with acceptance criteria and a retest path before handoff.
  • The client-safe note says what was reviewed, what changed and what still needs a full audit later.
  • If the workflow is too low-risk to justify paid QA, the report does not invent issues to protect the invoice.
